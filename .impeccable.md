## Design Context

### Users

FairHire AI is used by Responsible AI and model-risk teams, HR/TA leaders, legal and DPO reviewers, ML engineers, procurement teams, and internal or external auditors. They use it in high-consequence review workflows to register recruitment AI systems, inspect fairness and proxy risks, assign remediation work, approve releases, and export reproducible compliance evidence.

### Brand Personality

Calm, credible, exacting. The interface should create confidence without pretending certainty, make risk feel manageable rather than theatrical, and communicate the seriousness of an audit product without becoming bureaucratic or cold.

### Aesthetic Direction

A refined light enterprise workspace with warm paper-toned surfaces, deep ink green as the primary brand color, restrained amber for review states, and muted red for genuinely blocking risks. The visual language should feel closer to a modern financial control room or a carefully typeset audit dossier than a generic AI dashboard. Use editorial typography, thin rules, strong alignment, tabular numbers, and selective color. Avoid blue-purple AI gradients, neon-on-dark styling, excessive cards, glassmorphism, oversized rounded corners, and decorative charts without meaning.

### Design Principles

1. Evidence before score: show source, method, sample size, uncertainty, and status together.
2. Calm hierarchy: critical states are unmistakable, while routine information stays visually quiet.
3. Editorial precision: typography, alignment, and whitespace should make dense governance data readable.
4. Progressive disclosure: portfolio health first; methodology and raw evidence remain one interaction away.
5. Human accountability: owner, reviewer, deadline, and approval state should always be visible near a risk.
6. Accessible by default: WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, non-color status cues, reduced-motion support, and adaptive mobile layouts.
